# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Cloud recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Abdullahi Omer for early-career cloud roles. They should be able to review relevant work, understand his contribution, and open his résumé or contact him.

## Product Purpose

Present Abdullahi’s cloud development work in one place. Help visitors understand what each project does, which tools were used, what he contributed, and which results are supported by evidence.

## Positioning

The portfolio is itself an AWS hosting and delivery project. It also presents a team Azure reporting project and a simulated SpendWise browser demo. Abdullahi’s industrial design background and Phase 1 community work give context to his approach to cloud development.

## Operating Context

Visitors can scan Selected Work on the homepage and use the résumé and contact links when they want to follow up. SpendWise has a separate project site. The portfolio is a public static website.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The homepage presents the three selected projects with their images and descriptions. SpendWise is the only project with a link out from Selected Work.
- Preserve the distinction between personal contributions, team work, and disclosed AI assistance.
- Describe project status and verification using the available evidence. Mark plans and unverified results explicitly.

## Brand Commitments

Use the name Abdullahi Omer. Keep the writing clear and factual, with cloud development as the primary focus.

## Evidence on Hand

- `content.js` contains the project summaries, case-study details, and contact destinations.
- `assets/cloud-architecture.svg` illustrates the portfolio’s AWS hosting architecture.
- `assets/retail-dashboard.png` shows the Power BI report from the CST8911 team project.
- `docs/project-content-sources.md` documents source material and attribution.

## Product Principles

- Make the project and Abdullahi’s role easy to identify at a glance.
- Use actual project artifacts and keep claims tied to evidence.
- Make the résumé and contact routes easy to find.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep the portfolio usable on desktop and mobile and with keyboard navigation. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep project content readable when optional motion is unavailable.
